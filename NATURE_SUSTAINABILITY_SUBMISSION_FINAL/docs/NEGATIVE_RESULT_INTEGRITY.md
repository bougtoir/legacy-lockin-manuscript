# Negative-result integrity — PASS

- Were all null primary branches included? **Yes** — national and subnational
  agriculture, and both transition interactions, are reported in main text,
  Fig 2, Fig 4 and ED Table 3.
- Were any null results omitted from figures? **No** — both agricultural nulls
  and the H2 null are plotted.
- Were any positive secondary analyses promoted? **No** — the lag-1 and
  intensive-margin H1 estimates are labelled secondary in text, figure panels
  (physically separated panel) and ED Table 3; they are explicitly described as
  hypothesis-strengthening, not confirmatory.
- Were any definitions changed after outcomes? **No** — the capacity re-scope
  (V3→V4) was completed and locked *before* the single opening; documented in
  02_CAPACITY_SCOPE_COMPARISON.md of the transition package.
- Were confidence intervals represented symmetrically? **Yes** — all reported
  CIs are shown in full.
- Were nulls described as "no effect" when meaningful effects remain
  compatible? **No** — H1 is described as "directional but uncertain" with the
  CI shown to include large barriers; agricultural CIs are described as
  excluding large effects rather than proving zero.
- Were weak positives described more strongly than nulls? **No** — the urban
  effect, the only precise primary interaction, is explicitly classified
  "weak/context-dependent" and its non-replicating respecifications are
  enumerated in the main text.

Overall: **PASS**.
