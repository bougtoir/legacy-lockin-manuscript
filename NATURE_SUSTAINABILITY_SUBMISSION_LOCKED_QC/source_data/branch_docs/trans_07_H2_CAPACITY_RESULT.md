# 07 — H2 capacity result

**Frozen hypothesis:** prior FEMA transformational-departure programme
experience (departure-scope cumulative obligations) moderates the
flood-demand → first-entry relationship positively (capacity enablement):
β(demand × capacity) > 0.

**Estimate:** +0.0342 [−0.0975, +0.1660], p = 0.610 (county-cluster CRV1).

## Classification: **RESULT B — null**

- Point estimate is in the enablement direction but small and imprecise;
  p = 0.61 is far from any boundary.
- Capacity's *main* effect is large (deep-experience counties enter programmes
  at far higher baseline rates — see 05), but that is accumulated institutional
  experience predicting entry directly, not moderation of the demand response.
- Robustness: sign positive in 7/9 numeric variants but never significant
  (closest: state-cluster p = 0.49, logit p = 0.60, intensive secondary
  p = 0.052 in the *negative* direction — different estimand, see 09).
- Leave-one-state-out: H2 range +0.008 to +0.047, never significant; no
  influential state.

Not A (CI crosses zero), not C (would need significantly negative), not D
(stable sign and magnitude across variants).
